#+title: ANF
#+filetags: :Language:

ANF is a simple and effective IL for functional languages, alternative to CPS.
ANF is used in [[id:ba81ebf5-d895-4e7c-b075-b2f505060f5e][SydML]], coming after [[id:a6c6f6a6-84b3-4250-8ec2-27d27411b324][Core]], and before SSA.

* Further reading
- [[https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/compiling-without-continuations.pdf][Compiling without continuations]]
  Describes ANF and join points as used in GHC.
  - [[https://youtu.be/LMTr8yw0Gk4][SPJ's talk on the subject]].
  - Formalises join points with typing, operational semantics, and syntax.
  - Describes heavyweight optimisations for join points.
  - The paper's implementation allows for /recursive/ join points.
- [[https://legacy.cs.indiana.edu/~sabry/papers/essence-retro.pdf][Original ANF paper: The Essence of Compiling with Continuations]]
- [[https://compiler.club/compiling-lambda-calculus/][Colin — Compiling the λ-calculus]]
