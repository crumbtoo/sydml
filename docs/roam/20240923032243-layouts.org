#+title: Layouts

/Layouts/, inherited from Haskell, are a convenient and flexible basis for
whitespace-sensitive syntax. At the core, a production ~Layout S P~ parses token
~~S~~, followed by a sequence of ~P~ productions, following a few simple rules:

- Each ~P~ must be indented further than the leading token ~S~.
- All elements ~P~ must be aligned with one another; i.e., every ~P~ is equally
  indented.
- Lines indented further than the ~P~ elements are folded into the preceeding
  element.
