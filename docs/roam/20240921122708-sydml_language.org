#+title: SydML (Language)
#+filetags: :Language:

SydML is a bit of a misnomer. I was originally going to implement a subset of
OCaml, before opting for indentation-sensitive syntax and purity, leaving it
two steps away from Haskell.

* Syntax
- SydML features Haskell-style layouts, meaning that one can group productions
  by either alignment, or semicolons and braces.
